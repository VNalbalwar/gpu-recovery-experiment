GPU Recovery Project — Unified Final Analysis

1. DURING INTERFERENCE
Compute and L2 produce strong victim slowdowns. Memory's run median is near/below 1x, but its P95/P99 tails are elevated, so median alone understates its interference effect.

2. AFTER INTERFERENCE
Across the duration experiments, recovery run medians remain approximately 1.0x baseline for Memory, L2, and Compute. Recovery P95/P99 are generally only ~1.03–1.06x baseline. There is no clear increase in recovery penalty with longer interference duration.

3. EXTENDED L2
10 control trials and 10 L2-interference trials were analyzed at the trial level.
Control vs L2:
- median: 0.9865 vs 1.0000
- P95: 1.0905 vs 1.3192
- P99: 1.2174 vs 1.5523
- median fraction >1.2x: 1.5% vs 11.5%
The uncorrected tests show tail signals, but Holm-adjusted p-values are not statistically significant.

4. TEMPORAL L2
The ~2 ms extended window shows no reproducible monotonic decay. Median latency is essentially at baseline across bins; P95 fluctuates and remains elevated in some bins. This should be described as binned tail variability, not a continuous recovery curve.

5. FINAL SCIENTIFIC CLAIM
The experiments establish strong active interference but do not establish a reproducible persistent post-interference slowdown. L2 shows suggestive increased tail variability after interference, but the effect is heterogeneous and not statistically conclusive after multiple-comparison correction.

STATISTICAL UNIT
Trial-level inference is used for condition comparisons to avoid treating hundreds/thousands of recovery probes from the same trial as independent experiments.
